The Challenge & Gap

Why Was This Needed?

Traditional architectural workflows leave buyers confused and force developers to leak proprietary blueprints to third-party cloud APIs.

1. The Visualization Gap

Over 85% of home buyers and real estate clients cannot mentally translate 2D CAD floor plans into actual 3D volume, ceiling height, and room scale.

Buyer Frustration

2. Blueprint Privacy Risks

Architectural blueprints contain sensitive building layouts and security designs. Sending raw floor plans to third-party cloud APIs introduces compliance and data leakage risks.

Zero Cloud Egress Needed

3. Static, Unintelligent Plans

Static brochures and PDFs can't calculate natural daylight angles, evaluate ventilation pathways, or audit circulation bottlenecks before construction.

Missing Spatial Heuristics
The Innovation

What We Built

An interactive 3D WebGL spatial engine coupled with a local on-device AI architect powered by Google Gemma 4.

📐 Real-Time 3D WebGL Dollhouse

Converts architectural wall coordinates and room polygons into realistic 3D spatial models with floor materials, lighting, and orbit/first-person controls.

🖼️ Picture-in-Picture 2D Blueprint Overlay

Live collapsible CAD drawing overlay allowing architects and buyers to cross-reference flat schematics against the interactive 3D space in real time.

🤖 On-Device AI via Gemma 4 gemma4:e4b

Local Ollama integration analyzes daylight ratings, circulation efficiency, and room suitability on your GPU with zero cloud subscription or API keys required.

✓ Local Gemma 4 Audited
Technical Architecture

Under the Hood: Privacy & Performance
